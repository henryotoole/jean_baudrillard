## 2026-09-02 - The Kidney of the Mundane

When LLM's write docs, they invariably add details. So far I've found that the best way to prevent these details from piling up is to declare a place they can safely do so. `conventions.md` has traditionally been the place this occurs, and that's a bad place. 

They're going to land in ADR's now (or get quietly forgotten in operational docs). But either way, I think every LLM doc-writing system needs what I lovingly call:

*The Kidney of the Mundane*

A place for junk to end up that doesn't clutter critical context.
